Wedding Portal

A portrait of friendship and a pre-wedding trip to Venice, translated into fabric.

The story

Venice, before the wedding.

Brenda was about to get married when she and Kelly traveled to Venice for a bachelorette party of sorts. Photographs from that trip became the source material for Wedding Portal. Karen used those images to carry the memory of the trip into fabric, including the challenge of capturing Kelly's unmistakable '70s perm. The photograph of Brenda shown below was specifically cited as one of the inspirations for the finished piece.
